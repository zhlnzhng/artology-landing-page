We're thrilled to welcome you to the Bookology blog!

## Why We Built Bookology

Bookology was born from a simple observation: many readers love their books but struggle to maintain a consistent reading habit and keep track of their literary journey. We wanted to create something different—not another social network for books, but a personal sanctuary for your reading life.

### What Makes Bookology Different

**Focus on You, Not Others**

Unlike other book apps, Bookology doesn't have feeds, likes, or followers. It's designed to be a personal tool that helps you build and maintain your reading habit without the noise of social features.

**Beautiful & Minimal**

We believe that tracking your reading shouldn't feel like work. Every aspect of Bookology is designed to be intuitive and delightful to use, with a clean interface that gets out of your way.

**Your Data, Your Way**

With iCloud sync, your reading data stays private and secure. No algorithmic recommendations, no targeted ads—just your books and your notes.

## What to Expect from This Blog

Moving forward, we'll be sharing:

- **Reading tips and insights** to help you build better habits
- **Product updates** about new Bookology features
- **Behind-the-scenes** stories about building tools for readers
- **Guest posts** from fellow book lovers

If you haven't already, [download Bookology for iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6747273681?pt=120196342&ct=Blog&mt=8) and start building your personal library.

We can't wait to be part of your reading journey.
